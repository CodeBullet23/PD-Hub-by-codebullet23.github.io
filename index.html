📜 PD Rules & Expectations
Core policy • Must‑read
Policy live
These are the non‑negotiable rules for PD members. You can expand this list, but the structure keeps everything readable and easy to reference during training or disciplinary reviews.
Rule 01
Follow chain of command
All PD members must respect rank and follow the chain of command. Disputes or concerns go up the chain, not into public chat or in‑game arguments.
Command first • No public drama
Rule 02
Professional conduct on and off duty
You represent the department whenever you wear the uniform or use PD tags. No harassment, toxicity, or trolling—inside or outside the server.
Respect the badge
Rule 03
No abuse of powers
Using PD powers (tools, vehicles, permissions) for personal gain, trolling, or favoritism is an instant removal and may be logged on the Wall of Shame.
Zero tolerance • Abuse = out
Rule 04
Follow server rules and PD SOP
PD rules do not replace server rules—they stack. You must follow both the server rules and PD standard operating procedures at all times.
Server + PD rules together
