PD Hub — Master Department

The PD Hub serves as the central command platform for all authorized divisions within the jurisdiction. This system provides structure, clarity, and operational oversight for every department connected to our network. All activity routed through the PD Hub follows standardized protocols, ensuring consistency, accountability, and professional conduct across the entire enforcement and security framework.

This website is used for department access, application processing, operational status monitoring, rule enforcement, and communication of official updates. All divisions listed within the PD Hub are required to follow the Terms of Service (TOS), department rules, and chain-of-command expectations.

Violations of PD Hub rules or misuse of police/security tools may result in disciplinary action. Confirmed violations are logged publicly in the Tab of Shame, where users who break protocol may be listed along with the reason for their infraction. This ensures transparency, accountability, and a clear record of misconduct.

The PD Hub platform is actively maintained, and updates will continue to roll out as new features, departments, or operational improvements are introduced. Future enhancements may include expanded department controls, improved status indicators, additional rule modules, and upgraded command tools to support both police and security operations.

Thank you for using the PD Hub. Maintain professionalism, follow protocol, and operate responsibly under the authority of PD Hub Command.

Department Operations & Structure

This hub serves as the unified command point for all law‑enforcement subdivisions. All members are expected to operate professionally, follow RP standards, and maintain proper radio communication at all times.

🔐 Department Access System

City Patrol — Open Access
City Patrol is open to everyone. Any member may join and operate within city limits.

Waitlisted Departments
The following subdivisions require staff approval before joining:

  • County / Sheriff
  • Highway / State
  • Border Enforcement
  • Security

To join a waitlisted department, contact a staff member. They will direct you to the correct subdivision server.

📡 Communication Requirements

All departments must remain connected to the central communication channel for patrol updates, weather calls, and operational notices.

City Patrol members must still follow:

  • RP rules
  • Radio communication guidelines

If you are unfamiliar with radio codes, request assistance from staff.

📊 Jurisdiction Overview

  • City Patrol — City limits only.
  • County / Sheriff — County areas only.
  • Highway / State — Highway routes between city & county.
  • Border Enforcement — Border zone only.
  • Security — Independent operations but must follow community standards.

📁 Leadership Requirements

Leadership positions require professionalism, coordination with the main hub, and enforcement of subdivision standards.

🧭 Unified Alignment

All departments remain part of the unified structure to ensure smooth coordination, clear jurisdiction, and consistent operations.

Chain of Command

Chain of Command

The following structure outlines the official PD Hub hierarchy. All personnel must respect rank authority, follow RP standards, and maintain proper communication discipline.

👑 Chief of Police

Highest authority within the PD Hub. Oversees all subdivisions, operations, and leadership coordination.

🛡️ Deputy Chief

Second-in-command. Assists the Chief with administrative duties, supervision, and department alignment.

⭐ Captain

Manages division-level operations, supervises Lieutenants, and ensures compliance with hub standards.

🎖️ Lieutenant

Oversees Sergeants and daily patrol operations. Handles incident oversight and field coordination.

🟧 Sergeant

Supervises Corporals and Officers. Responsible for shift management, patrol assignments, and discipline.

🟦 Corporal

Senior field officer responsible for mentoring junior staff and maintaining patrol quality.

🟩 Senior Officer

Experienced officer trusted with advanced duties, training support, and operational stability.

🚔 Officer

Standard patrol unit responsible for responding to calls, enforcing laws, and maintaining RP integrity.

🪪 Probationary Officer

Entry-level position. Must follow training guidelines, radio rules, and supervisor instructions.

Specialized Units

🟩 Border Patrol Unit

Handles border-zone enforcement, checkpoint operations, and cross-jurisdiction coordination.

🟦 Highway Patrol Unit

Responsible for highway safety, traffic enforcement, and roadway incident response.

🟧 State Trooper Unit

Operates statewide enforcement duties, high-risk patrols, and inter-agency coordination.
